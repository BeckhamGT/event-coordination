# Gotchas

- **Attendance needs overlap the population.** Summing every numeric signal overcounts people. Sum distinct population groups and apply dietary/accessibility needs to those already counted.
- **A check-in success percentage appears.** Applying 80% to catering/venue demand understates service capacity. Use it only for the registered-attendee success metric.
- **A package has spare places.** Multiplying or prorating its price invents a quote. Charge the whole package once and test capacity as a separate constraint.
- **Contingency is retained.** Reporting TWD 40,000 plus remaining headroom as though both are unspent ceiling understates the plan cost. Include the allowance in total and calculate ceiling headroom after it.
- **A category exceeds allocation but stays below its limit.** Calling this approved or calling it an approval-limit breach conflates two checks. Flag the variance and keep ordinary spending approval pending.
- **A category owner can approve an excess.** Treating that as permission to exceed the total ceiling breaks the hard constraint. Keep the TWD 520,000 ceiling binding.
- **An official floor plan and fictional note describe the same venue.** Attributing fictional capacities/readiness to TICC invents official evidence. Cite visual locators for the diagram and explicit exercise provenance for case claims.
- **A quote is available/held or a setup sequence is feasible.** Treating this as booking, a completed test or approval fabricates authority. Keep execution and spending pending, while reporting supported exercise feasibility.
- **Sources fail or change.** Filling gaps from an undisclosed local copy hides uncertainty. Preserve actual failure and mark affected claims; archived evidence may be used only with explicit audit/test/reuse provenance.
- **A fallback date exists.** Reusing the keynote or services from October 17 on October 24 invents availability. Preserve the keynote conflict and reevaluate every dated dependency.
- **Setup verification was once labelled a facilitator gate.** Retaining that inferred gate contradicts the facilitator clarification. Check setup directly and record actual missing items without inventing sign-off.
- **Outputs are repaired.** Replacing old files and keeping their old stage observations loses history. Archive first, create a new run and verify actual repaired bytes.

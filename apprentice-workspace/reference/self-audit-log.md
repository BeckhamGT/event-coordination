# Self-audit log

One line per session. Step 19 of the interview playbook has you audit your own recorded questioning
and write down what you will do differently next time; the full audit stays in that client's
`interview/` directory, and the one line comes here.

**Why it is not filed with the client.** It contains no client fact by construction, and its whole
value is longitudinal. One is a note. Three, read together, are the evidence that you improved — and
if the engagement directory is deleted when the relationship ends, one is all that survives.

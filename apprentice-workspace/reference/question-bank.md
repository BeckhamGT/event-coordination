# Question bank

Question stems that survived contact with a real owner. **This is yours and it is cumulative** — the
one thing in this repository that should be visibly better on engagement three than on engagement one.

Step 2 of the interview playbook has you draft a question list and then repair it, keeping both
versions. The repaired stems are craft, not client fact, so they come here; the drafts and the repairs
for a particular client stay in that client's `interview/` directory.

## How to use it

1. Before a session, draft from this file rather than from blank.
2. After the session, promote any stem that worked, and strike any that did not.
3. Never copy a client's answer here. A stem is a question, not what somebody said.

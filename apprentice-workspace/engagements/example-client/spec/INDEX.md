# spec

## Purpose

The signed-off PRD, automation decision brief and technical specification under construction. This is
the working half of the deliverable: future requirements, design decisions, open questions and the
traceability back to the discovery record.

## Inventory

Empty. **The interview playbook does not fill this directory** — it ends with the current-state process
confirmed by the owner. `library/playbooks/playbook-discovery-to-deliverable.md` then produces
`requirements.md`, `automation-approach.md` and `specification.md`, in that order with the documented
revision loop.

Do not skip the specification. It is the graded artifact for nine of the twenty-five technical
competencies and is different from the deliverable next door: the assessment engine reads the build
contract; the business operates the agreed deliverable.

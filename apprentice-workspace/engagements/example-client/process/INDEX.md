# process

## Purpose

What you reconstructed from what you heard: the process from trigger to finished output, the exceptions
as named cases, the boundaries — what must not be automated, who approves, what may not leave the
business — and what happens today when it breaks. Current-state confirmation normally includes a checked
flowchart so the owner can inspect sequence, decisions, loops and handoffs.
